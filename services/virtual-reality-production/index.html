Virtual Reality & Immersive Experience Production

Immersive environments for training, simulation and marketing, built from the same source models as the visualization work so geometry and materials stay consistent across outputs.

What we deliver

  • VR environmentsHeadset-ready walkthroughs of spaces that do not exist yet, or are unsafe to visit.
  • Training and simulationProcedure rehearsal in a controlled environment with repeatable scenarios.
  • AR overlaysOn-site augmented views aligning proposed geometry with the existing condition.
  • Marketing experiencesShowroom and event pieces that let a visitor move through the product.

How a project runs

  1. Scope and data review. We look at what already exists — drawings, models, GIS or sensor data — and agree what the output has to prove and who it is for.
  2. Model build. Geometry is built or cleaned against the source data, at the level of detail the deliverable actually requires.
  3. Production. Rendering, interaction or analysis is produced against the agreed scope, with review points rather than a single reveal at the end.
  4. Handover. Final deliverables are issued in the agreed formats, with source files available on request.

Who it is for

Developers, operators and training teams that need people to experience a space or procedure rather than look at pictures of it.

Common questions

Which headsets are supported?

Experiences are built for current standalone and tethered headsets. The target device is agreed up front, since it sets the performance budget.

Can you reuse our visualization model?

Yes. Reusing the source model keeps geometry and materials consistent between renders and the VR build.

Is this useful for training as well as marketing?

Yes. Procedure rehearsal in a repeatable, controlled environment is one of the clearest uses of VR.
