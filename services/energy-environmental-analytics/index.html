Energy & Environmental Analytics

Analysis that sits on top of the model rather than beside it: solar exposure, energy performance and environmental effects evaluated against the same geometry used for design and visualization.

What we deliver

  • Solar and shading studiesAnnual and seasonal solar exposure across a site or facade.
  • Energy efficiency analysisPerformance comparison between design options on a consistent basis.
  • Emissions monitoringReporting views built from operational and sensor data.
  • Sustainability reportingOutputs prepared for planning submissions and stakeholder reporting.

How a project runs

  1. Scope and data review. We look at what already exists — drawings, models, GIS or sensor data — and agree what the output has to prove and who it is for.
  2. Model build. Geometry is built or cleaned against the source data, at the level of detail the deliverable actually requires.
  3. Production. Rendering, interaction or analysis is produced against the agreed scope, with review points rather than a single reveal at the end.
  4. Handover. Final deliverables are issued in the agreed formats, with source files available on request.

Who it is for

Planners, architects and asset owners who need sustainability and performance evidence early enough to still influence the design.

Common questions

At what stage should this start?

As early as possible. The value of performance analysis is in changing the design, which gets harder the later it arrives.

Do you work on an existing model?

Yes, where the geometry is suitable. Analysis sits on top of the same model used for design and visualization.

What outputs are produced?

Solar and shading studies, option comparisons, and reporting views prepared for planning submissions and stakeholders.
