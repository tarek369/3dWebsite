Urban & Infrastructure Digital Twins

A digital twin is a dynamic, high-fidelity virtual replica of a physical asset. It lets you test, simulate and understand real-world scenarios — from seasonal weather to operational efficiency — before anything is built.

What we deliver

  • GIS-aligned city modelsTerrain, buildings and infrastructure built against real coordinate data rather than arbitrary geometry.
  • Scenario simulationCompare proposed interventions against the existing condition in the same model.
  • Monitoring viewsDashboards and visual states driven by live or periodic data feeds.
  • Planning and consultation mediaAnimations and interactive views used to explain proposals to stakeholders and the public.

How a project runs

  1. Scope and data review. We look at what already exists — drawings, models, GIS or sensor data — and agree what the output has to prove and who it is for.
  2. Model build. Geometry is built or cleaned against the source data, at the level of detail the deliverable actually requires.
  3. Production. Rendering, interaction or analysis is produced against the agreed scope, with review points rather than a single reveal at the end.
  4. Handover. Final deliverables are issued in the agreed formats, with source files available on request.

Who it is for

Municipalities, urban planners, infrastructure operators and consultancies that need to evaluate options before committing capital.

Common questions

How is a digital twin different from a 3D model?

A 3D model shows how something looks. A digital twin is built against real coordinate data and is designed to be queried and simulated against — it answers questions, not just renders.

What source data do you work from?

GIS datasets, survey and terrain data, CAD and BIM where available, and sensor feeds where the twin needs to reflect live conditions.

Can we compare proposed changes against what exists today?

Yes. Scenarios are modelled in the same environment as the existing condition so the comparison is like-for-like.
