AI-Enhanced Urban Intelligence & IoT Management

Sensor networks produce more data than anyone reads. We connect that data to a spatial model, apply analytics on top, and present it as something a planner or operator can act on.

What we deliver

  • IoT sensor integrationIngesting device and sensor feeds into a single spatial model.
  • AI-enhanced analyticsPattern detection and derived indicators across combined data sources.
  • Centralised management viewsOne interface across systems that are usually siloed by vendor.
  • Spatial dashboardsLive state shown in the model's geometry rather than in an abstract chart.

How a project runs

  1. Scope and data review. We look at what already exists — drawings, models, GIS or sensor data — and agree what the output has to prove and who it is for.
  2. Model build. Geometry is built or cleaned against the source data, at the level of detail the deliverable actually requires.
  3. Production. Rendering, interaction or analysis is produced against the agreed scope, with review points rather than a single reveal at the end.
  4. Handover. Final deliverables are issued in the agreed formats, with source files available on request.

Who it is for

Smart-city programmes, infrastructure operators and municipal teams running sensor deployments that have outgrown per-vendor dashboards.

Common questions

Do we need to replace our existing sensors?

No. The work is integration — bringing existing device and sensor feeds into one spatial model.

What does the AI layer actually do?

Pattern detection and derived indicators across combined feeds, so operators see conditions rather than raw streams.

Can this sit on top of a digital twin?

Yes, and it usually should. The spatial model is what makes the data readable.
