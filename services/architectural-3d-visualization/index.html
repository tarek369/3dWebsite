Architectural 3D Visualization & Media Production

Photoreal renders, animated walkthroughs and presentation media that show a building as it will actually be experienced — light, materials, landscape and context — long before construction starts.

What we deliver

  • Still visualizationHigh-resolution exterior and interior stills for planning submissions, investor decks and marketing material.
  • Animated walkthroughsCamera-led fly-throughs and walk-throughs that communicate spatial sequence and scale.
  • Environment and light studiesSeasonal, daylight and weather variations rendered from the same model so design decisions can be compared directly.
  • Presentation mediaBoards, loops and social cutdowns prepared from the same asset set.

How a project runs

  1. Scope and data review. We look at what already exists — drawings, models, GIS or sensor data — and agree what the output has to prove and who it is for.
  2. Model build. Geometry is built or cleaned against the source data, at the level of detail the deliverable actually requires.
  3. Production. Rendering, interaction or analysis is produced against the agreed scope, with review points rather than a single reveal at the end.
  4. Handover. Final deliverables are issued in the agreed formats, with source files available on request.

Who it is for

Architecture practices, property developers, real-estate marketing teams and competition entries that need the design understood by a non-technical audience.

Common questions

What do you need from us to start?

Whatever exists: CAD or BIM files, sketches, site photos, material references. We can work from 2D drawings alone, though a model shortens the schedule.

Can you show the building in different seasons or lighting?

Yes. Once the model is built, daylight, seasonal and weather variations are rendered from the same scene, so options stay directly comparable.

What formats do we receive?

High-resolution stills, video walkthroughs and cutdowns for presentation or social use. Source files can be handed over on request.
